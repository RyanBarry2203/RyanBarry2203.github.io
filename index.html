EA Best Use of AI · Games Fleadh 2026

Pale Roots

A sci-fi roguelike on a custom 2D engine, where you use the power of dead enemies to grow stronger.

Role
Solo developer
Tech
C#, MonoGame
When
Dec 2025 to Mar 2026

  1. Award-winning enemy AI

    Pale Roots won the EA-sponsored Best Use of AI award at Games Fleadh 2026. I built the whole game solo in MonoGame, with no commercial engine underneath.

  2. Armies that spread out instead of swarming

    Every unit runs a small state machine (Wander, Chase, Combat, Hurt) behind an IAIState interface. Targeting is centralised: every 500 ms, units without a valid target pick the nearest enemy that has fewer than two attackers already, so fights break into duels rather than one big pile.

  3. An engine built from scratch

    A pushdown stack of game states handles menus, cutscenes and level-ups. Combat broadcasts C# Action events, so the UI and audio listen without the combat code knowing they exist. Arenas are 60×34-tile maps, filled procedurally from data.

Research · with paper

Hybrid LOD Traffic Simulation

City traffic that runs as full Unity GameObjects near the camera and as Burst-compiled ECS data everywhere else, handing each car back and forth without a seam.

Role
Solo, self-directed
Tech
Unity 6, DOTS / ECS, Burst, C#
Output
Playable build and paper

  1. The problem

    A hundred cars with rigidbodies, raycasts and Update loops chokes the main thread and spikes the garbage collector. Most of those cars are too far away for the player to see.

  2. Hydrate near, dehydrate far

    Inside the hydration radius, a car is a pooled GameObject with sensor and decision modules. Past the larger dehydration radius, it becomes an ECS entity with three components, moved kinematically by a Burst job. Each road node has the same integer ID in both worlds, so a hand-off is just "where am I, where am I going, what's left of my A* path".

  3. Tools and write-up

    I wrote a Waypoint Sync editor tool that mirrors the GameObject road network into the DOTS subscene, plus a one-click "make roads two-way" tool. The paper compares pure OOP, pure ECS and the hybrid in the Unity Profiler.

About

Right now I'm tech lead on Split_Shift, a team open-world racing game, where I own the racer and police AI. Outside college I've managed a busy cocktail bar for years, so I'm used to leading a team under pressure.
